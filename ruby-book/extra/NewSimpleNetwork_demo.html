Configuration

One XP crosspoint switching a single CHI channel between four peers (North, East, South, West). Every peer has a TX (traffic generator) and an RX (consumer); each direction is its own credited link: credit spent at send, message flies t_link cycles into the input CLB, dwells ≥ 1 cycle, and the credit returns to the sender at arbiter grant + t_creditReturn. Messages are coloured by destination. Things to try (or use the presets): drop one link's credits below RTT_min = t_link + 1 + t_creditReturn and watch its throughput cap at credits/RTT_min · freeze an RX and watch credit starvation walk upstream · point all TX at East to create a hot-spot · turn off HoL elimination while an RX is frozen and watch other flows starve behind blocked heads — then raise staging depth to win some slack back.
Legend: →North →East →South →West credit (filled = available at sender; moving back = returning over t_creditReturn) · slot row on a wire into the XP = that input's CLB · slot row inside the XP at an output = staging · slot row inside a peer = its RX CLB · yellow flash = arbiter grant · red = stalled · each wire is tagged with its latencies: t_link (forward, grey) and t_cr = t_creditReturn (gold); t_pipe inside the XP
