Spike runs the same RISC-V workloads two orders of magnitude faster than gem5's fastest functional CPU. This page walks through the fourteen patches that recover 6.5× of that gap without changing what the guest sees: for each one, the problem in the per-instruction loop, how the patch removes it, why guest behaviour stays identical, and what it measured.
